About me

I'm a front-end development student with an interest in creating websites that combine clean design with practical functionality. I enjoy exploring how design and code work together to bring ideas to life, from the overall layout to the smaller details that make a website feel complete. Each project gives me an opportunity to experiment, learn something new, and become more confident in my skills.

I'm always looking for ways to improve my work, whether that's learning new techniques, solving problems, or finding better ways to approach a design. I believe that the best results come from a balance between creativity, curiosity, and a willingness to keep learning. Through the project in my portfolio, I hope to show both my development as a front-end developer and the kind of work I'm passionate about creating.
